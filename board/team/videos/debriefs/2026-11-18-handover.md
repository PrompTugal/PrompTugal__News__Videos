## What I learnt
- The team already held much of the evidence: social.watch@'s `coverage.json` (Website repo) and, from 18 Nov, the library's catalogue. Read before collecting.
- Two rules bind the collection: websummit.com is not read by machine, and nothing new is built on Web Summit's data (Dinis, 11 Nov); linking its pages is fine, copying is not (Dinis, 18 Nov). Talks come from YouTube's chapters, never the agenda.
- Anyone who publishes in public is named by the name they published under, never with private details (Dinis, 18 Nov 15:35).
- The Bash tool turns `\u` escapes typed in a command into the real characters; a literal U+2028 inside a regular expression broke the build twice. Build such escapes from character codes in a script file, and check with `grep -P '[^\x00-\x7f]'`.
- The leak scan flags hex longer than 47 characters and signatures; manifests keep 40 hex, and signatures wait for Dinis.
- Check relayed facts against the primary source: Google's quota and restriction pages corrected three points of the relayed key instructions.
- The library withdraws items (12 withdrawn in the 22:49 UTC read, 10 of them since 16:49; none a video); the merge now honours `status` and takes a withdrawn video off this site.
